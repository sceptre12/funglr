About Funglr

Funglr is a small microblogging app in the spirit of Tumblr, built with AngularJS. Members can publish text, image and audio posts, follow each other, and like, comment on or reblog what they see.

Everything runs in your browser: your account and posts are kept in browser-local storage, so they are still there when you reload the page.

What you can do

  • Create an account and sign in.
  • Share text, image and audio posts from your dashboard.
  • Follow other members and read their posts in your feed.
  • Like, comment on and reblog posts.
